# Exploring Duala vocabulary from parallel sentences

Run `python linguabridge/scripts/build_duala_vocabulary.py` at the repository root. The generated `duala-vocabulary-research.json` indexes each distinct casefolded Duala surface form. `occurrences` counts tokens; `sentence_count` counts sentence pairs; `example_pair_indices` are zero-based indices into the `entries` array of `duala-english-parallel-dictionary.json`. Read both full sentences before attributing meaning to a word.

The index retains attached tone/diacritic marks and internal apostrophes. Distinct spellings, inflections, punctuation conventions, OCR issues and borrowed words can all create separate surface forms. It is **not** a count of distinct dictionary lemmas. English cooccurrences are ranked by observed/expected sentence cooccurrence, with a minimum of three shared sentence pairs. They are **not translations or alignments**: common Duala particles regularly get misleading rare English associations. No entry is certified for finance, and this file must not be loaded into the production translator.

To color and analyze a sentence, choose an `example_pair_indices` value, show the English and Duala sentences side by side, color repeated occurrences of the selected Duala form, then annotate the English span manually with a confidence level and sentence reference. Record grammar observations separately from lexical meaning. Have a fluent speaker check morphology, tone and financial use before promoting anything to an active lexicon.
